# Contributing to Cyclo

Thank you for your interest in Cyclo. Cyclo is proprietary software with all
rights reserved (see [LICENSE](LICENSE)). Its source code is public so it can
be read and discussed, not because the project is open to unsolicited
contributions.

## Contributions are by invitation only

- Contributions are accepted **by invitation only**. Before preparing or
  submitting any contribution (code, designs, texts, translations or other
  material), you need explicit permission from the copyright holder,
  Gabriel Martinho ([@gaebriu](https://github.com/gaebriu)).
- An invitation only allows you to prepare and submit the contribution it
  covers. It does not grant any other right over Cyclo.
- The copyright holder has full discretion over whether a contribution is
  accepted or rejected, and may reject any contribution without giving a
  reason.
- Pull requests opened without an invitation may be closed without review.

Bug reports, questions and discussion are welcome where the repository
offers them; they are not contributions in the sense above.

## Who owns a contribution

- **You keep the copyright** over your own original contribution, unless you
  and the copyright holder agree otherwise in a separate written agreement.
- Submitting a pull request, or having one accepted and merged, does **not**
  transfer copyright in either direction: it does not give you any ownership
  of Cyclo or its existing materials, and it does not give the copyright
  holder ownership of your contribution.
- Cyclo does not use a Contributor License Agreement (CLA) and does not ask
  for any assignment or transfer of intellectual property rights.
- Under Portuguese law, your moral rights as author (for example, to be
  recognised as the author of your contribution) remain yours.

## Permission to use your contribution

Because you keep the copyright, Cyclo can only include your contribution if
you allow it. Before a contribution is merged, you and the copyright holder
agree in writing (for example, in a comment on the pull request) on the
permissions needed to incorporate, adapt and distribute that contribution as
part of Cyclo and its official releases. This is a permission for that
specific contribution, not an assignment of rights and not a CLA. If no such
permission is agreed, the contribution is not merged.

Note that, under the GitHub Terms of Service (section D.6), content added to
a repository that contains a license notice is, by default, licensed under
that same license. Cyclo's license reserves all rights, so that default does
not give the project the permissions above; this is why they are agreed
explicitly for each contribution.

## Your responsibilities

- Only submit material you created yourself or have the right to submit, and
  say clearly if any part comes from a third party, and under which license.
- Respect Cyclo's proprietary license: an invitation to contribute does not
  allow you to copy, reuse or redistribute Cyclo's code or assets elsewhere.
- Follow the project's conventions (see the existing code and documentation).
